Lab 2: Multivariate Visualization with D3

This visualization explores the relationship among population, average temperture, development level, and region of the city.

Design Justification

I mapped population to the x-position and the average temperature to the y-position because both variables are numerical and their relationship can be easily compared in the two dimensional cahrt. I used color to represent region because region is a nomial variable with four categories, and color is a good way to distinguish them. I used circle size to represent development level because it is an ordinal variable with a natural order from Low to High. This visualization allows viewers to easily compare the relationship between population and average temperature, while also seeing differences across development levels and regions.
